# Mechanical Parts - Covers

This is for Mechanical parts that are primarily covers for something.

## Car Seat Bolt Cover

What do you do when you discover that your used car is missing a plastic bolt cover?

I have ordered replacement parts like this from dealers and not only are the parts
expensive, they have high shipping costs.

In this case on my used VAN when be bought it, we did not notice that the plastic cap on
on onr og the arm rests that covered the bolt that held the arm rest on was missing.

This turns out to be one of the simplest designs to create in FreeCAD and then 3d
print.

I used a package of small roles of multiple colored filaments intended for a 3d
pen to find a color that closely matched the seat.
