# Mechanical Parts - Covers

This is for Mechanical parts that are primarily covers for something.

## Car Seat Bolt Cover

What do you do when you discover that your used car is missing a plastic bolt cover?

I have ordered replacement parts like this from dealers and not only are the parts expensive, they have high shipping costs.

In this case on my used VAN when be bought it, we did not notice that the
plastic cap on on onr og the arm rests that covered the bolt that held the
arm rest on was missing.

This turns out to be one of the simplest designs to create in FreeCAD and
then 3d print.

I used a package of small roles of multiple colored filaments intended for a
3d pen to find a color that closely matched the seat.

## Door Threshold Screw Covers

Noticed that some of these were missing on the door thresholds, and have no
idea if they can be purchased separately.   This started out as similar to
the Car Seat Bolt Cover.   The first version lasted about a year and then I noticed they had broken.  The "_r1" version uses a 100% infill for part of
cover that does not reach the bolt and appears to be a lot stronger.

I could not match the color of the originals exactly.  However a missing
cover is very noticeable, Covers with a bit different color than the
threshold are not.

## Pet Food Lids

We do not feed a full service of the wet pet food for each feeding, so we need
a lid to cover it.   Lids eventually crack and fail and not all brands provide
a lid.   I tried ordering some lids from an online store, while they were
very cheap, they also were slightly too small to fit well.

So I created my own simple lids.

The size can be adjusted in the FreeCad models.

These lids were printed with TPU filament.
